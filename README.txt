Control your Glue smart locks from Homey. Log in once with your Glue account and every lock in your account shows up as a device with lock and unlock, battery level, online status and the latest lock event. Status is refreshed every minute and a few seconds after each command.

Use the Lock all doors action in a flow, for example every evening. After about 20 seconds the app checks that every lock really locked and can tell you about any lock that is offline or did not lock. Your password is only used once to create an API key and is never stored.
